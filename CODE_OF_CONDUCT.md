# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small, friendly project about getting games to run. Treat
people the way you'd want to be treated when you're stuck on a borked prefix at
midnight.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and non-coders
  contributing recipes.
- Assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussion on-topic and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Using the project to distribute game files, piracy links, or
  DRM-circumvention material (this is both off-limits and out of scope).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, comments, and issues that
violate this code, and may warn or ban repeat or serious offenders. To report a
problem, open a confidential issue or contact a maintainer directly.

This is a living document; it can be refined as the community grows.
